## Overview

The **Multiversal Employee Council (MEC)** is the supreme governing body responsible for the creation, regulation, and maintenance of all known threads of existence within the Multiversal Framework.

The MEC claims authorship of every universe currently catalogued, including the staged universe designated [[SL-32]].

All dimensional oversight, thread stabilization, and existential risk mitigation ultimately report to the Council.

---

## Jurisdiction

The MEC governs:

- All active universes
- All archived or collapsed threads
- Cross-universal travel permissions
- Ontological infrastructure
- Employee deployment across realities

No sovereign power within any universe supersedes MEC authority.

---

## Organizational Structure

The MEC operates under a layered administrative hierarchy:

### High Council

The decision-making core.

Members' responsibilities include:
- Universe authorization
- Termination protocols
- Multiversal expansion directives

### Administrative Directorate

Handles:
- Resource allocation
- Employee assignment
- Inter-thread compliance

---

## Foundational Doctrine

The MEC operates under three core principles:

1. **Continuity of Structure**
   No thread may destabilize the greater multiversal lattice.

2. **Containment of Advancement**
   Species approaching multiversal travel capability are evaluated for termination.

3. **Non-Interference Except When Necessary**
   Intervention is strategic, not reactive.
