## 1. Overview

M-VERSE is a structured multiversal framework composed of discrete **Universal Fields**, interconnected through controlled interfaces and governed by a substrate known as **Quotient**. Unlike naturally evolved multiverses, M-VERSE includes both natural and curated universes, with active intervention by Operators (MUO) and Employees (MEC).

This document serves as a complete reference for the physics, architecture, and operational logic of M-VERSE.

## 2. Universal Fields (Field Stratification Model)

### 2.1 Definition

A **Universal Field** is a bounded, selective-permeability layer that determines what forms of existence, causality, and structure may persist within a universe.

Fields are **nested**, forming an onion-like structure around each universe.

---

### 2.2 Primary Fields (Minimum Configuration)

#### Alpha Field (Α) — Reality Interface Layer

- Permits matter, energy, time, and observers
- Governs classical physics, relativity, and observable quantum effects
- Humans exist exclusively within Alpha

#### Beta Field (Β) — Structural Constraint Layer

- Enforces conservation laws and causality
- Suppresses direct Quotient expression
- Stabilizes constants and timelines

#### Gamma Field (Γ) — Existential Boundary Layer

- Separates universes from one another
- Interfaces with the Void
- Accumulates Quotient at high-tension depths

---

### 2.3 Advanced Fields (Optional)

#### Delta Field (Δ) — Directive Layer

- Allows controlled reality modification
- Accessed by World Cubes and MEC Employees

#### Epsilon Field (Ε) — Abstract Constraint Layer

- Regulates narrative causality and inevitability

#### Zeta Field (Ζ) — Identity Preservation Layer

- Maintains continuity of self across transitions

---

### 2.4 Field Depth Index (FDI)

Fields are gradients, not discrete shells. Depth is indexed numerically:

- Α.1 → Α.10 (surface to threshold)
- Γ.01 → Γ.99 (boundary gradients)

FDI is used for portal routing, hazard mapping, and equipment calibration.

**Quotient** is a pre-structural substrate beneath matter and energy. It enables the rearrangement of atoms, probability, and causality through directive intent rather than force.

## 11. Organizational Roles

### 1. Anonymous (Origin Authority)

Anonymous is not a person, AI, deity, or organization in any human sense.

- No confirmed form (usually using a humanoid manifold to project for communication and interactions)
- No chain of command
- No observable intervention patterns

Anonymous represents the **origin layer** of the M‑VERSE.

All authority *ultimately* traces upward to Anonymous, but **Anonymous does not issue orders**. Instead, the system appears to be self‑regulating beneath them.

Anonymous is neither benevolent nor malicious. He simply *exists*, and by existing, permits everything else to exist.

### 2. Multiversal Employee Council (MEC)

**Role:** Supreme Administrative Authority

The MEC are often referred to—incorrectly—as *Multiversal Gods*. In truth, they are **maintainers**, **administrators**, and **architects** of reality.

### Responsibilities

- Creation of universes
- Deletion, rollback, or quarantine of universes
- Approval of major interventions
- Final arbitration during corruption events
- Oversight of *all* departments

### Authority Model

- Absolute jurisdiction
- Cannot be overruled by any other entity
- May override reality constraints directly

The MEC rarely acts directly. Most of their work is **delegative**, intervening only when corruption, systemic failure, or existential threats arise.
