Hudson Clease, better known as *The Time Traveler* is a highly skilled human ally of the [[MEC]], possessing a time dilation booth for the assistance of travelling and conversing the time and reality dimensions.  

## Appearance

Hudson is a Human male, at the time of writing, 15 years of age, of average height and build. With the following attire:

### Headwear

They wear a wide brim, cowboy style of hat, with a bell sized peacock feather in it.

### Upperwear

They wear a white, button-up short-sleeved shirt, completed with a chest pocket on the left side.

### Bottomwear
They wear blue, ripped jeans, with faded knees, two shallow pockets lacking buttons. Additionally with a grey belt with a golden accent
## The booth

The booth, better known as a "*Time Machine*" is a large, human size device capable of controlling and harnessing the power of [[Time Serum]] 

This booth was inherited from [[Merlin]] during an invasion of Merlin's watchtower
